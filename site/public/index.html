Meet Athanor.

How it organises, files, searches and blocks — in a minute and a quarter.

What it does

Everything open,
nothing in the way.

The things you reach for every day, a key press away, and calm the rest of the time.

Tidy every tab in one press CtrlShiftF

Loose tabs move into folders by site. Undo puts them back.

Spaces, folders, vertical tabs

Pinned sites stay on top. Each space keeps its own tabs. Idle tabs archive themselves.

One bar for everything you type CtrlK

Addresses, searches, open tabs, commands, and developer tools such as JWT decoding.

Blocking, built into the engine

Brave's adblock-rust stops ads, trackers and YouTube ads. Nothing to install.

Every tab at a glance CtrlSpace

Tabs you haven't opened yet stay unloaded.

Side by side Ctrl\

Any number of panes. Drag the dividers.

Design

Boards

A canvas for images and notes, kept on top while you work. Opens PureRef boards.

Quiet in light. Quieter in dark.

Themes are JSON, and every part of the interface can be restyled by extensions.

Made for the way you build

Local dev servers appear on the start page. Downloads and media controls live in the address bar. DevTools are one key away.

Light by design

No bundled Chromium.Tabs load when you open them.Idle ones step aside.
